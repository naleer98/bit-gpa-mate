# Handbook Rules Used in the Calculator

Based on the supplied University of Moratuwa CODL BIT Student Handbook — 2024 curriculum.

## Programme levels and awards

- Level 1 — Diploma Level
- Level 2 — Higher Diploma Level
- Level 3 — Degree Level

## Credit and minimum LGPA checks

- Level 1: 30 GPA credits and minimum Level 1 LGPA 2.00
- Level 2: 30 GPA credits, 3 compulsory NGPA credits, and minimum Level 2 LGPA 2.00
- Level 3: 30 GPA credits (18 compulsory + 12 elective) and minimum Level 3 LGPA 2.00

## GPA and class

- SGPA: credit-weighted grade points for one semester
- LGPA: credit-weighted grade points for the corresponding level
- Degree class: based on Level 3 LGPA, subject to official timing and other conditions

## Disclaimer

The app is a planning calculator. Official qualification eligibility and academic standing are decided using University records and the relevant academic authorities.
