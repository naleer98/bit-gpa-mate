Grade points & repeat rules

Grade scale

MarksGradeGrade pointMeaning

Important rules

  • D or above earns credit for a course module.
  • CA minimum: 35% is required to sit the semester examination.
  • Semester exam minimum: 35% is required for D or above.
  • F: repeat CA + semester exam; repeat grade is capped at C.
  • I: repeat semester exam only; improvement is capped at C.
  • T: absent after completing CA; next semester exam is treated as a first attempt.
  • Degree class: determined using Level 3 LGPA, not overall CGPA.

GPA formula

GPA = Σ (Course credits × Grade point) ÷ Σ Course credits

T and X have no grade point and are excluded until a GPA-bearing result is available.

About this project

Made for BIT students

BIT GPA Mate is an offline-first, student-friendly calculator based on the supplied University of Moratuwa CODL BIT 2024 curriculum handbook screenshots.

It is designed for planning and self-checking only. Final academic decisions always come from official university records and the Board of Examiners.

Privacy

Your grades stay inside your browser using local storage. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Works offline

After the first load, the site can work without internet in supported browsers.

Install as an app

Use your browser menu and choose “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”.
