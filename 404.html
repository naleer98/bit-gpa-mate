Must know

Important rules

  • D or above earns course credit.
  • Minimum LGPA 2.00 is required at each level for its qualification.
  • Level 1: 30 GPA credits for Diploma requirements.
  • Level 2: 30 GPA + 3 NGPA credits for Higher Diploma requirements.
  • Level 3: 30 GPA credits for BIT Degree requirements.
  • F: repeat CA + semester exam; repeat grade capped at C.
  • I: repeat semester exam only; improvement capped at C.
  • T: next exam sitting is treated as a first attempt.
  • Degree class: based on Level 3 LGPA, subject to official conditions.

Weighted calculation

GPA formula

SGPA uses one semester. LGPA uses the corresponding level modules.

GPA = Σ (Credits × Grade Point) ÷ Σ Credits
